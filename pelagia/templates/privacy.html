This policy explains what Pelagia collects and how it is used when you log and share scuba dives.

What we collect and why

We store your public username, Google account identifier and email address, plus the profile photo, bio, certification, dive details, locations, photos, buddy tags, likes, and comments you provide. We use this information to manage your account, show your logbook and statistics, and provide the app’s social features.

If you choose Google sign-in, we receive and store your Google account identifier and email address to sign you in or link your account. Your sign-in email is not shown in profiles or user search. Google access tokens are not saved.

Who can see your information

Your profile, dives, notes, photos, buddy tags, likes, and comments can be seen by other signed-in users. Each active dive automatically has a public preview showing your username, avatar, site, location, date, dive type, center, photos, map, depth, and duration. A preview exists even if you never send its link.

Uploaded photos can also be opened without signing in by anyone with their file URL. Avoid uploading sensitive information or other people’s personal details without permission. Copies saved by other people or cached by sharing services may remain after you remove content.

Cookies and services

A session cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days after your last request; using the app renews it. Logging out clears the session. Blocking cookies prevents sign-in. The installed app caches public interface assets and an offline screen, not your dive log or photos.

If you choose email sign-in, Resend processes your email address and delivers a one-time code. We store your verified sign-in address privately, plus temporary verification records and a keyed hash of the requesting IP address for abuse prevention. Verification records are removed after one day when new codes are requested.

Hosting providers process information needed to deliver the service and may log IP addresses and browser requests. Maps load tiles from OpenStreetMap, which receives your IP address and requested map area. Google sign-in is handled by Google. See the OpenStreetMap privacy policy and Google privacy policy.

Pelagia does not use advertising or analytics trackers, or sell your personal information.

Storage and your choices

Account information and content are stored on the server while retained by the service; there is no automatic expiry. You can edit your profile and dives and remove photos while editing a dive. Deleting a dive hides it from the app and public previews, but retains the stored record and attached files. Account deletion and complete erasure are not currently self-service features.

Contact the operator below to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information. Your rights depend on applicable law. Passwords are stored as hashes, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Policy updates
