Pelagia on your phone

Add to Home Screen

Add Pelagia to your home screen from your browser.

iPhone & iPad

Open Pelagia in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen (look under More if needed). Leave Open as Web App enabled if shown, then tap Add.

Android

Open Pelagia in Chrome. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app, and confirm.

On your computer

In Chrome or Edge, use the install icon in the address bar or choose Install Pelagia from the browser menu. In Safari on Mac, choose File → Add to Dock when available.

Visit Pelagia over HTTPS to install. Open the new icon and sign in with Google. An internet connection is needed to view and save dives.
