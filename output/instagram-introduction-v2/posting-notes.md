## Production notes

Images edited with the built-in image generation tool. The full edit prompts are saved in prompts.json. Underwater scenes are generated campaign imagery.

“Latest reported conditions” reflects the current implementation: site conditions are aggregated from the latest day of logged dives within the past seven days. They are not a live sensor or weather feed.
