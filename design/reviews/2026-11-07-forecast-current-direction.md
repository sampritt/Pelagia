# Forecast current direction

Forecast now places current direction immediately after strength, beneath a centered Current heading spanning two equal-width columns. Current and wind directions share the same neutral text, compass ring, arrow geometry, spacing, and focus behavior. Per the [Open-Meteo Marine API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/marine-weather-api), current direction points toward the flow; wind remains a from-direction with its arrow reversed to show travel. Both current cells open the existing current chart.

`ocean_current_direction` is requested with the other hourly and current marine fields and stored in the existing per-site cache. The 15-minute lifetime, local-midnight expiry, worker leases, retry behavior, and browser cache remain unchanged. Direction validates degree units and the 0–360 range, joins forecast slots by timestamp, and interpolates circularly across north in fractional-offset timezones. Missing values and older cached payloads show a dash without affecting other metrics; normal cache refresh supplies the new field. Loading placeholders cover all nine columns without changing table or tide height.

Browser review used local deterministic data in Chrome at 320, 360, 390, 430, 768, 1024, and 1440px widths, 844 × 390 landscape, metric and imperial units, and 125% text. Reviewed the full page, horizontal metric scrolling, sticky time column, header centering, compass styling, date and site controls, tide chart, navigation, loading, partial data, unavailable/retry, pending responses, featured/single/empty states, chart destinations, and Back restoration. Site-tag computed styles and focus remain identical to Explore, and touch emulation confirms independent site scrolling. No horizontal page overflow, cell-content overflow, loading layout jump, or browser errors were detected. The card remains reachable above mobile navigation at the bottom of the page.
